# Frame Mode

Use frame mode before building or redesigning a meaningful interface.

## Discovery

Answer enough of these to avoid generic design:

- Who is using this surface?
- What job are they trying to finish?
- What data, decisions, or emotional state do they bring?
- What must be visible immediately?
- What can be progressive, secondary, or omitted?
- What existing design patterns in the repo should be preserved?
- Is this a product or brand surface?
- What would make this feel wrong for the audience?

## Brief

Produce a short buildable brief:

- `Surface`
- `Audience`
- `Primary job`
- `Register`
- `Information priority`
- `Interaction model`
- `Visual direction`
- `States required`
- `Verification plan`

Do not implement from an unconfirmed brief when the request is high-stakes,
broad, or visually open-ended. Ask for confirmation or present two to three
clear alternatives.
