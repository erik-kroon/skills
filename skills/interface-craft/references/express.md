# Express Mode

Use express mode to intentionally change the personality, intensity, color, or
ambition of an interface.

## Submodes

- `bolder`: increase impact and memorability.
- `quieter`: reduce aggression and make the design more refined.
- `color`: add strategic color and visual hierarchy.
- `delight`: add small moments of personality or joy.
- `signature`: add technically ambitious moments where the surface can justify
  the complexity.

## Register Rules

- Product surfaces earn expression through clarity, speed, confidence, and
  precise detail.
- Brand surfaces can carry more atmosphere, imagery, color commitment, and
  narrative rhythm.
- Do not make everything louder or quieter; choose the elements that matter.
- Do not let delight hide state, cost, risk, or task progress.
- Ambitious effects need progressive enhancement, reduced-motion behavior, and
  performance verification.

## Color Strategy

Choose one before changing values:

- Restrained: tinted neutrals plus one accent.
- Committed: one dominant color carries identity.
- Full palette: several named roles used deliberately.
- Immersive: the whole surface is shaped by the color world.

## Output

- `Submode`
- `Register`
- `Current intensity`
- `Target intensity`
- `Changes`
- `Verification`
