# Amplify Mode

Use amplify mode to intentionally change the expressive intensity of a design.

## Submodes

- `bolder`: increase impact and memorability.
- `quieter`: reduce aggression and make the design more refined.
- `colorize`: add strategic color and visual hierarchy.
- `delight`: add small moments of personality or joy.

## Rules

- Gather audience, brand, and use-case context first.
- Do not make everything louder or quieter; choose the elements that matter.
- Keep usability and hierarchy intact.
- Delight comes after clarity.
- Quiet does not mean generic.
- Bold does not mean chaotic.

## Output

- `Submode`
- `Current intensity`
- `Target intensity`
- `Changes`
- `Verification`
